Learn Knowledge Graphs with Phoebe

Entities and relations as a graph, queries over it, and graph-based retrieval. Find the questions only a graph can answer, and when a GraphRAG is worth its cost. Six sessions follow one constructed publisher, Vellum Press, whose research desk asks questions several hops deep. The graph is real: a Wikidata extract around a thousand Nobel laureates, their doctoral advisors, universities and countries. The centre is a lab that answers 24 questions two ways in your browser, by walking the graph and by retrieving text chunks, and counts what each one finds at every hop depth.

The six sessions 🎯 · from "how many hops is this?" to graph, vectors or both

The running case is Vellum Press, a constructed academic publisher whose desk answers questions like "which laureates studied under someone who also won?". Each session adds one section to Vellum's graph design note, and the last one turns it into a costed decision.

🔗 Session 1 · foundations

When the question is about relationships

Entity, relation and hop; the questions a table answers and the ones a graph answers; join depth as the tell; what chunk retrieval quietly gets wrong. Build-along: sort ten desk questions by hops.

▶ Start here 🧩 Session 2 · foundations

Nodes, edges and two models

The labelled property graph and RDF triples, IRIs and Wikidata's statements, qualifiers and Q-numbers; GQL as an ISO standard; schema first or schema later. Build-along: Vellum's graph model.

the graph model 🔎 Session 3 · core

Cypher and SPARQL, side by side

MATCH patterns and variable-length paths, SPARQL graph patterns and property paths, the same question in both. Build-along: four queries over the laureates, run in the page.

four queries 🎛️ Session 4 · bench night

The multi-hop lab

24 questions answered by graph traversal and by BM25 chunks, computed live: recall by hop depth, context cost, and the missing relation that fails without an error. Build-along: the multi-hop result.

★ the bench 🕸️ Session 5 · applied

Paths, centrality and communities

Shortest paths, degree, betweenness and PageRank, community detection, and the hub that wins every measure because of how the graph was modelled. Build-along: who connects Vellum's network.

the network finding 🤖 Session 6 · capstone

GraphRAG: when the graph feeds the LLM

Graph as context, community summaries, text-to-Cypher, the cost of extraction and upkeep, and the evaluations that disagree about the gains. Capstone: Vellum's graph, vectors or both decision.
